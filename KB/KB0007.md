# Access: reset a forgotten PVP Corp password
Category: Access

## Symptoms
You forgot your `pvpcom.internal` Windows login password or cannot sign into `https://myapps.pvpcom.com` after returning from leave.

## Cause
Your Active Directory password was forgotten or expired while offline, preventing SSO authentication across `@pvpcom.com` services.

## Steps
1. On a separate device or phone browser, go to `https://passwordreset.pvpcom.com`.
2. Enter your full `username@pvpcom.com` email address and complete the CAPTCHA prompt.
3. Select **Approve a notification on my PVP-Guard MFA app** (or choose **SMS code** to your registered mobile number in Workday).
4. Enter a new password that meets PVP Corp complexity rules and click **Finish**.
5. If working remotely, connect to **PVP-SecureConnect** using the new password, then lock (**Win + L**) and unlock Windows to sync cached credentials.

## If it still fails
Call the **PVP IT Service Desk** hotline (`+1-800-555-0199` or internal ext. `4357`). Have your `PVP-EmpID` and manager's name ready for live identity verification.
