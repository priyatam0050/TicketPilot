# Access: unlock a locked Active Directory or SSO account
Category: Access

## Symptoms
Windows login displays "The referenced account is currently locked out and may not be logged on to," or `sso.pvpcom.com` blocks sign-in attempts.

## Cause
Five consecutive failed authentication attempts occurred within 15 minutes—commonly triggered by a mobile mail app or mapped network drive holding an old password after a reset.

## Steps
1. Wait **15 minutes** for PVP Corp's automatic Active Directory lockout timer to expire without attempting further logins.
2. While waiting, close Outlook on your phone or update your saved password in **Settings > Mail > Accounts > PVP-Exchange**.
3. If you need immediate access before 15 minutes, open `https://passwordreset.pvpcom.com` on your phone, select **Unlock Account**, and verify via **PVP-Guard MFA**.
4. Once unlocked, sign into Windows and clear stale entries in **Windows Credential Manager**.

## If it still fails
Contact the **PVP IT Service Desk** (`ext. 4357`) or ask a peer to log a ServiceNow ticket under **PVP Identity > Account-Lockout** so an analyst can trace the source IP (`Event ID 4740`) locking your account.
