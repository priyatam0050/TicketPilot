# Network: guest Wi-Fi setup for office visitors
Category: Network

## Symptoms
An external vendor, interviewee, or client visiting a PVP Corp office cannot connect their personal laptop or phone to `PVP-Corp-5G` and needs temporary internet access.

## Cause
`PVP-Corp-5G` requires a domain-joined `PVP-8021X-MachineCert` certificate; non-employee devices must use the isolated `PVP-Guest` captive portal sponsored by a host employee.

## Steps
1. On your employee laptop, open `https://sponsor.pvpcom.internal` and sign in with your `@pvpcom.com` SSO credentials.
2. Click **Create Visitor Pass**, enter the guest's full name, company, mobile number, and select duration (`24 Hours` or `5 Days`).
3. Click **Generate Voucher** to display a 6-digit `PVP-GST-XXXXXX` access code.
4. Instruct the visitor to connect to the `PVP-Guest` Wi-Fi SSID, open any browser to load the `guest.pvpcom.com` splash page, and enter the voucher code.

## If it still fails
Contact the local **PVP Workplace Front Desk** or submit a ServiceNow ticket under **PVP Network > Guest-Portal-Support** with the visitor's MAC address and host `PVP-EmpID`.
