# Hardware: request a new laptop or accessory
Category: Hardware

## Symptoms
Your current `PVP-LT-*` laptop has reached the 36-month **PVP-Refresh** cycle, or you need an approved headset, ergonomic mouse, keyboard, or `PVP-Dock-G4`.

## Cause
All hardware assets and peripherals require manager and cost-center approval in ServiceNow before warehouse dispatch.

## Steps
1. Open the **PVP ServiceNow Portal** at `https://support.pvpcom.internal` and sign in with **PVP-Guard MFA**.
2. Click **Hardware Catalog** and choose **Standard Laptop Refresh** (for devices over 36 months old) or **IT Peripherals & Accessories**.
3. Select an approved item (e.g., `Jabra Evolve2 65 Headset` or `Logitech MX Master 3S`), verify your shipping address or office pickup desk, and click **Order Now**.
4. Track approval and courier dispatch emails sent from `servicenow@pvpcom.com`.

## If it still fails
Raise a ServiceNow inquiry under **PVP Workplace > Asset-Logistics** (or call `ext. 4357`) referencing your `REQ` number and cost center code.
