# Network: troubleshoot slow internet or lagging video calls
Category: Network

## Symptoms
Web pages take over 15 seconds to load, **PVP-Chat** screen sharing freezes, or audio breaks up while connected to office Wi-Fi or home VPN.

## Cause
Split-tunnel routing is disabled on your active VPN profile, routing heavy video streams through the `vpn-gw.pvpcom.internal` concentrator, or **PVP-OneSync** is saturating upload bandwidth.

## Steps
1. Open **PVP-SecureConnect** and verify you are connected to `PVP-SSL-ZeroTrust` (which split-tunnels video traffic) rather than `PVP-FullTunnel-Audit`.
2. Right-click the **PVP-OneSync** cloud icon in the system tray and select **Pause syncing > 2 hours**.
3. Open a browser and run an internal throughput test at `http://speedtest.pvpcom.internal` (if on VPN/office) to check latency (`< 60 ms` expected).
4. Close background browser tabs running cloud builds or large downloads and retest your call.

## If it still fails
Submit a ServiceNow ticket under **PVP Network > NetOps-Performance**. Attach a screenshot of your `speedtest.pvpcom.internal` result and note whether you are on `PVP-Corp-5G`, wired dock, or home VPN.
