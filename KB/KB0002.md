# Access: set up PVP-Guard MFA on a new phone

Category: Access

## Symptoms

You upgraded or replaced your smartphone and no longer receive **PVP-Guard MFA** push notifications when signing into `myapps.pvpcom.com` or **PVP-SecureConnect**.

## Cause

MFA device tokens are cryptographically bound to your old phone's hardware keystore and do not transfer automatically via iCloud or Android backups.

## Steps

1. Download and install the **PVP-Guard MFA** app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store on your new phone.

2. If you still have your old phone, sign into `https://myapps.pvpcom.com/security-info` on your laptop, approve the prompt on the old phone, and click **Add sign-in method > PVP-Guard App**.

3. Scan the QR code displayed on your laptop screen using the **PVP-Guard MFA** app on your new phone, then delete the old device entry.

4. If you no longer have your old phone, click **I can't use my PVP-Guard app right now** and verify via your backup SMS number to register the new device.

## If it still fails

Call the **PVP IT Service Desk** (`ext. 4357`) to request a temporary **TAP (Temporary Access Pass)** after verifying your `PVP-EmpID` and manager approval.
