# Access: request access to a shared network drive
Category: Access

## Symptoms
You try to open `\\pvp-fs01.pvpcom.internal\Finance` or another department share and receive "Windows cannot access... You do not have permission to access this share."

## Cause
Your Active Directory account is not a member of the `SG-FS-<Dept>-RO` or `SG-FS-<Dept>-RW` security group mapped to that NTFS folder.

## Steps
1. Open **PVP-AccessHub** at `https://accesshub.pvpcom.internal` and sign in via `sso.pvpcom.com` with **PVP-Guard MFA**.
2. Click **Request Access > Shared Folders** and search for the exact UNC path (e.g., `\\pvp-fs01.pvpcom.internal\Marketing`).
3. Select **Read-Only (`RO`)** or **Read-Write (`RW`)**, enter your business justification, and click **Submit**.
4. Once the folder data owner approves the `RITM`, lock (**Win + L**) and unlock your `PVP-LT-*` laptop—or run `gpupdate /force`—to refresh your Kerberos token.
5. Open File Explorer and map the folder under **Map network drive**.

## If it still fails
Submit a ServiceNow ticket under **PVP Identity > FileShare-ACL** (or call **PVP IT Service Desk** at `ext. 4357`). Include the full `\\pvp-fs01...` path, your `RITM` number, and your `PVP-EmpID`.
