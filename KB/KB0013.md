# Access: access removal and offboarding for leavers
Category: Access

## Symptoms
An employee or contractor is leaving PVP Corp, and their manager needs to ensure immediate revocation of VPN, email, and `sso.pvpcom.com` access.

## Cause
Standard offboarding disables accounts at 18:00 local time on the final working date entered in Workday, requiring manual escalation for immediate or involuntary exits.

## Steps
1. For planned resignations, confirm HR has entered the final working date in **Workday** (`https://workday.pvpcom.com`).
2. For immediate or sensitive terminations, the manager must open **ServiceNow > PVP Identity > Immediate Access Revocation**.
3. Enter the leaver's `PVP-EmpID` and select **Terminate Active Sessions Now** to revoke `sso.pvpcom.com` tokens, disable **PVP-SecureConnect**, and block **PVP-Guard MFA** within 5 minutes.
4. Collect the leaver's `PVP-LT-*` laptop and `PVP-Badge`, then ship the hardware using the prepaid **PVP-AssetReturn** kit.

## If it still fails
Call the **PVP IT Service Desk** (`ext. 4357`) or the **PVP Security Operations Center (SOC)** (`ext. 9911`) immediately to force-disable the Active Directory account and block mailbox forwarding.
