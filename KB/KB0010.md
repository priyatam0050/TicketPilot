# VPN: first-time setup on a PVP Corp laptop
Category: Network

## Symptoms
You are working remotely for the first time on a newly issued `PVP-LT-*` laptop and cannot reach `intranet.pvpcom.internal`, Jira, or network file shares.

## Cause
The **PVP-SecureConnect** client has not yet pulled your user-level SSL certificate (`PVP-UserVPN-Cert`) from the regional gateway.

## Steps
1. Connect your laptop to a stable home Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot.
2. Open the Windows Start menu, launch **PVP-SecureConnect**, and click **Add Gateway Profile**.
3. Enter `vpn2.pvpcom.com` as the server address and name the profile `PVP-SSL-ZeroTrust`.
4. Click **Connect**, sign in with your `username@pvpcom.com` credentials, and approve the **PVP-Guard MFA** prompt on your registered phone.
5. Wait 30 seconds for the tray icon to turn solid green before opening internal apps.

## If it still fails
Log a ServiceNow incident under **PVP Network > NetOps-VPN-Tier2**. Provide your `PVP-LT-XXXXX` asset tag, your city/country location, and any error code shown in **PVP-SecureConnect**.
