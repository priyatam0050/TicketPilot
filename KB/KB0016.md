# Hardware: add an office PVP-PrintAnywhere printer
Category: Hardware

## Symptoms
You need to print a document inside a PVP Corp office, but no corporate printers appear in your Windows or macOS print dialog.

## Cause
The universal **PVP-PrintAnywhere** pull-print queue (`\\pvp-ps01.pvpcom.internal\PVP-FollowMe`) has not been installed on your laptop profile.

## Steps
1. Ensure your laptop is connected to `PVP-Corp-5G` or **PVP-SecureConnect**.
2. Open the Start menu and launch **PVP AppStore** (Windows) or **PVP Self Service** (macOS).
3. Search for `PVP-PrintAnywhere Universal Driver` and click **Install**.
4. Once installation completes, print any document and select **PVP-FollowMe on pvp-ps01** as the destination printer.
5. Walk up to any Ricoh or HP multifunction printer on your floor and tap your employee `PVP-Badge` on the card reader to release your job.

## If it still fails
Submit a ServiceNow ticket under **PVP Workplace > Print-Support** with your `PVP-LT-XXXXX` asset tag, building code (e.g., `BLD-HYD-02`), and `PVP-Badge` serial number.
