# Access: PVP Corp password policy, complexity rules, and expiry
Category: Access

## Symptoms
Your password reset attempt at `https://passwordreset.pvpcom.com` is rejected with "Password does not meet complexity requirements," or you received an expiry warning email from `iam-noreply@pvpcom.com`.

## Cause
PVP Corp enforces a 90-day rotation policy and blocks passwords matching your previous 12 passwords or containing company dictionary words.

## Steps
1. Ensure your new password is at least **14 characters** long.
2. Include at least three of the following four character types: uppercase letters (`A-Z`), lowercase letters (`a-z`), numbers (`0-9`), and symbols (`!@#$%^&*`).
3. Do not include your first name, last name, `PVP-EmpID`, or banned terms such as `pvp`, `pvpcom`, `welcome`, or the current month/year.
4. Change your password before the 90-day deadline via `https://myapps.pvpcom.com > Profile > Change Password` while connected to `PVP-Corp-5G` or **PVP-SecureConnect**.

## If it still fails
Submit a ServiceNow ticket under **PVP Identity > IAM-AD-Support** with a screenshot of the exact rejection banner (do not include your password text in the ticket).
