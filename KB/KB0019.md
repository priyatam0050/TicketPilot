# Hardware: swollen laptop battery safety procedure
Category: Hardware

## Symptoms
Your laptop's trackpad is lifting or hard to click, the bottom aluminum case is bulging outward, or the laptop wobbles on a flat surface.

## Cause
Degraded lithium-ion battery cells have vented gas inside the sealed battery pouch, creating a thermal and fire safety risk.

## Steps
1. **Stop using the laptop immediately.** Save active files to **PVP-OneSync** or Git and shut down Windows/macOS completely.
2. Unplug the USB-C charger; **never** leave a swollen battery connected to external power.
3. Do not press on the bulge, puncture the chassis, or pack the laptop into a backpack or standard shipping box.
4. If in an office, carry the laptop carefully to the nearest **PVP TechBar** for placement in a fireproof lithium containment drum; if remote, place it on a non-flammable tile or concrete surface away from heat.

## If it still fails
Call the **PVP IT Service Desk** (`ext. 4357`) immediately or submit a Priority-1 ServiceNow ticket under **PVP Workplace > Safety-BatteryHazard** to arrange a loaner laptop and hazardous-goods courier pickup.
