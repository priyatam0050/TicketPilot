# Hardware: clear a printer paper jam
Category: Hardware

## Symptoms
A **PVP-PrintAnywhere** Ricoh or HP multifunction printer displays "Paper Jam in Tray 2" or "Misfeed in Fuser Unit" and stops releasing print jobs.

## Cause
Curled, damp, or overfilled A4/Letter paper stock has caught inside the feed rollers or duplexing assembly.

## Steps
1. Read the printer's touchscreen prompt to identify the exact jammed zone (e.g., `Tray 2`, `Right Cover A`, or `Duplex Unit`).
2. Open the indicated tray or side access panel gently without forcing latched plastic levers.
3. Grasp the jammed sheet with both hands and pull straight out along the paper path so torn scraps do not remain on the optical sensors.
4. Fan a fresh stack of paper, load it below the `MAX` fill line in Tray 2, and close all panels firmly until the screen displays **Ready**.

## If it still fails
Log a ServiceNow ticket under **PVP Workplace > Print-Support**. Include the printer hostname on the front label (e.g., `PRN-BLD02-FL3-N`) and the displayed error code.
