# Hardware: PVP Corp laptop won't turn on
Category: Hardware

## Symptoms
Pressing the power button on your `PVP-LT-*` Dell Latitude or `PVP-MBP-*` MacBook Pro produces no screen image, no fan spin, and no keyboard backlight.

## Cause
Static charge buildup on the motherboard embedded controller or a power delivery handshake fault from the `PVP-Dock-G4` docking station.

## Steps
1. Disconnect the laptop from the `PVP-Dock-G4` dock, external monitors, and all USB accessories.
2. Plug the original 65W or 90W USB-C charger directly into the laptop's primary charging port and confirm the charging LED illuminates.
3. For Windows `PVP-LT-*` laptops, hold the power button down for a full **25 seconds** to drain residual static, release, then press once normally.
4. For MacBook Pro (`PVP-MBP-*`), hold **Control + Option + Shift (right) + Power** for 10 seconds, then press Power.

## If it still fails
Visit your building's **PVP TechBar** walk-up desk or submit a ServiceNow incident under **PVP Workplace > Laptop-BreakFix** (or call `ext. 4357`) with your `PVP-LT-XXXXX` asset tag.
